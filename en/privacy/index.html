Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a translation; the Turkish version prevails.

The Nereye Koydum? app respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Data Controller

The data controller for this app is Gürcan Çimenay. Contact: gurcancimenay@gmail.com

Data We Collect

Only the item names, locations and notes you enter are stored. If you do not sign in, this information stays on your device only. If you sign in with Apple, your email address is also received. No data is collected for advertising or tracking.

How Data Is Used

Your data is used only to back up your items and let you access them on more than one device. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Processors

When you sign in, your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Sign-in is handled by Apple. Data is not shared with anyone else.

Voice Input

Voice input works only when you tap the microphone button. What you say is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition service, and audio may be sent to Apple for this. Audio is not sent to or stored on our servers.

Security

Session data is kept in your device's secure storage. Your items in the cloud can be accessed only with your own account; connections are encrypted.

Deleting Your Data

Profile → "Hesabı Sil" (Delete Account) permanently deletes your account and all your items in the cloud and on this device. Items saved without signing in exist only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Contact

For privacy questions and requests: gurcancimenay@gmail.com
