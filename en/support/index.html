Support

A problem or a suggestion? Write to gurcancimenay@gmail.com and I will get back to you as soon as I can. The app is in Turkish; tab names are shown in quotes below.

Do I need an account?

No. Adding, searching, editing and deleting items work without signing in and without internet. In that case your items stay only on your phone.

How do I back up my items?

Sign in with Apple on the "Profil" (Profile) tab. Your items are backed up to the cloud and appear on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

I changed phones. What happens to my items?

If you were signed in on the old phone, sign in with the same Apple ID on the new one and your items come back. Items saved without signing in exist only on the old device.

How do I delete my account?

"Profil" → "Hesabı Sil" (Delete Account) → "Evet, Sil" (Yes, Delete). Your account and all your items in the cloud and on this device are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

Voice input does not work

The app needs microphone and speech recognition permission. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Nereye Koydum? and turn both on. Voice input listens only when you tap the microphone button.

Where is my data and who sees it?

See the Privacy Policy. In short: no ads, no tracking, your data is not sold.
